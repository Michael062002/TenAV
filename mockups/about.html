About us

Welcome to Ten Technology Group

Welcome to Ten Technology Group — home to three specialist brands, TenAV, TenSignage and TenUnify — a UK-based designer, installer and support partner for audio visual, digital signage and workplace analytics. Established in 2003, we've been delivering workplace technology projects to organisations across the UK for over twenty three years: more than 4,000 projects, over 600 customers, and engineers based across the country. And a fair number of rooms we've been called in to fix after somebody else built them.

Photo PHOTO PLACEHOLDER — engineer commissioning a meeting room installation, on a UK customer site 16:9

At Ten Technology Group we're as passionate about getting a room right as the people who'll use it every day. Our goal is to give you advice that isn't tied to any one manufacturer, so nobody here has a reason to sell you the wrong thing. Our customers range from a single six-person huddle room to organisations running technology across dozens of UK sites, and what suits one is rarely what suits the other. We work across the leading audio visual, signage and analytics manufacturers, and twenty three years of specifying rooms gives us a clear view of where the money is well spent, and where it quietly isn't.

Because audio visual, digital signage and workplace analytics all sit under one roof, you get one contract and one account manager who knows your sites, rather than a different supplier for every screen, sign and support call. Projects can be bought outright, or spread across a monthly Technology as a Service subscription, so a multi site rollout doesn't have to wait on a capital budget round. Our engineers and account managers offer straightforward, independent advice you can act on, tailored to your building, your budget and how your teams actually work.

*We're part of Kubus Group, so when a project needs networking, connectivity or infrastructure sorted alongside the room itself, that's a conversation rather than a new procurement exercise. And we support everything we supply for as long as you have it — kit rarely fails on the day it's installed, it fails eighteen months later, and we're still the number you call.

Tell us what you're trying to do

One meeting room, a portfolio of sites, or support for kit somebody else installed: tell us what you're trying to do. We'll tell you what we'd do and roughly what it costs, and it commits you to nothing.
