Independence

Advice that isn't tied to a brand

No manufacturer sets our targets, so nobody here has a reason to sell you the wrong thing. What suits a six person huddle room and what suits a boardroom that doubles as a training space are rarely the same kit, or the same manufacturer. Sometimes the answer is the obvious brand. Often it isn't. Twenty three years of specifying rooms teaches you where the money is well spent, and where it quietly isn't.

Single point of contact

One relationship instead of five

Most organisations end up with a supplier for screens, another for signage, another for support, and a spreadsheet to track them all. We replace that with one contract, one account manager who knows your sites, and nowhere for the blame to go. When something stops working you call a person rather than filling in a portal.

How we work

Four things that don't change from project to project

People before product

We ask how a space actually gets used before recommending anything to put in it. Skip that question and you end up with a room full of features nobody touches.

Simple by design

Good kit gets out of the way. Complexity costs you twice: once to install, and again every time someone can't get a meeting started.

Independent by default

We'll tell you when the cheaper option is better, and when you don't need the upgrade at all. Saying no to a sale is what makes the yes worth something.

In it after handover

Kit rarely fails on the day it's installed. It fails eighteen months later, on the morning of something important. We support what we supply, for as long as you have it.

Group structure

Part of Kubus Group

We're part of Kubus Group. We bring the workplace technology; they bring IT, connectivity and infrastructure. So when the room job turns out to need the network sorting first, that's a conversation, not a new procurement exercise.

Tell us what you're trying to do

One meeting room, a portfolio of sites, or support for kit somebody else installed: tell us what you're trying to do. We'll tell you what we'd do and roughly what it costs, and it commits you to nothing.
