About the event

See what one accountable partner looks like.

Ageing systems. Failing networks. A disjointed way of working.

If your infrastructure is quietly slowing your teams down as the business grows, you already know how much of your own time goes into managing it.

Most organisations end up with separate suppliers for security, network infrastructure, meeting rooms and door entry, then spend their week holding the gaps between them together.

Following Kubus Group's acquisition of Tentechnology and AMCOMM, we now cover both sides of the data port, from the network behind the wall to the screen in front of it.

Come and see what that looks like in practice.

Ongoing support

One team, long after install day.

Installed. Commissioned. Signed off. Then what?

Most technology problems don't start on day one. They start eighteen months in, when a room stops working properly and nobody can agree whether it's the screen, the codec, or the switch behind the wall.

You don't have to work that out yourself. We monitor and support your network and your rooms together, with the same single point of accountability after we've installed it as during.

24/7 monitoring

We watch both sides of the port continuously. Most issues are picked up and resolved before your teams notice anything is wrong.

One service desk

One UK based team for AV and IT. Report it once and we own it through to resolution, whether the fault sits in the screen in front of the wall or the circuit behind it.

Preventative maintenance

Scheduled maintenance across the whole estate, plus rapid response against agreed SLAs when something does go wrong.

Reporting and optimisation

Clear data on what's being used and what isn't, so your next investment is built around how your business actually works.
