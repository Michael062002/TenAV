What is digital signage?

Digital signage uses commercial grade displays, such as LCD and LED screens, to deliver dynamic content to your audience, including employees, customers and visitors.

Unlike a printed poster, you can change it remotely, schedule it and run it 24/7. That might be a single digital display, a bank of digital screens or an LED video wall. It’s the same idea at a different scale.

That flexibility helps businesses communicate information, promote services, support wayfinding and create more engaging environments. It can be used across retail, hospitality, healthcare, education and corporate workplaces, among others.

Single digital display

  • Update in seconds

    Change content remotely. No reprints, no site visits.

  • Runs to a schedule

    Playlists switch by time of day, automatically.

  • Keeps visitors informed

    Calling, notices and news make waits feel shorter.

Bank of digital screens

  • Sized to your wall

    Tile 2×2, 3×3 or larger to fit the space.

  • One image, edge to edge

    Slim bezels carry one picture across every panel.

  • Many sources at once

    Split into live feeds, dashboards and promotions.

LED video wall

  • Truly seamless

    No bezels or joins, in any size or shape.

  • Bright in any light

    Stays vivid in sunlit atriums and shopfronts.

  • Built for 24/7

    Long-life modules, serviced panel by panel.

Wayfinding kiosk

  • Self-service directions

    Visitors search and get a route in seconds.

  • Frees up your team

    Routine questions answered on screen.

  • Insight from every tap

    See what people look for, and when.
