About Ten Technology Group

One partner for audio visual, digital signage and workplace analytics

Ten Technology Group has been designing, installing and supporting workplace technology for UK organisations since 2003. More than 4,000 projects, over 600 customers, engineers across the UK. And a fair number of rooms we've been called in to fix after somebody else built them.

We trade as three brands: TenAV for audio visual, TenSignage for digital signage, TenUnify for managed services and workplace analytics. Customers range from single site businesses to multinationals, and installations from one huddle room to full experiential builds.

Independence

Advice that isn't tied to a brand

We're an authorised reseller for most of the major manufacturers, which is exactly why we can be straight with you about them. No single badge to defend, and no manufacturer setting our targets, so nobody here has a reason to sell you the wrong thing.

What suits a six person huddle room and what suits a boardroom that doubles as a training space are rarely the same kit, or the same manufacturer. Sometimes the answer is the obvious brand. Often it isn't. Twenty three years of specifying rooms teaches you where the money is well spent, and where it quietly isn't.

Saying no to a sale is what makes the yes worth something.

Simplicity

One relationship instead of five

Most organisations end up with a supplier for screens, another for signage, another for support, and a spreadsheet to track them all. We replace that with one contract, one account manager who knows your sites, and nowhere for the blame to go. When something stops working you call a person rather than filling in a portal.

Built in house

We like this stuff more than is strictly normal, and it shows up in odd places. Where we couldn't buy something that did what our customers actually needed, we built it. Our own hardware and software, developed and tested in house, running on live sites today.

What we stand for

Three things we don't compromise on

01

People first

Technology should support the person using it, not get in the way. We ask how a space actually gets used before recommending anything to put in it, because no two rooms are the same. Complexity costs you twice: once to install, and again every time someone can't get a meeting started.

02

Connected thinking

The best outcome comes from systems that talk to each other. A screen that can't see the room booking system is just a screen, and an AV install that ignores the network becomes somebody's problem six weeks later. We look at what surrounds the job, not only the job.

03

Dependability

Kit rarely fails on the day it's installed. It fails eighteen months later, on the morning of something important. We support what we supply for as long as you have it. We keep tightening our processes and documentation so the answer doesn't depend on who picks up the phone.

Group

Part of Kubus Group

We're part of Kubus Group. We bring the workplace technology; they bring IT, connectivity and infrastructure. So when the room job turns out to need the network sorting first, that's a conversation, not a new procurement exercise.

Tell us what you're trying to do

One meeting room, a portfolio of sites, or support for kit somebody else installed: tell us what you're trying to do. We'll tell you what we'd do and roughly what it costs, and it commits you to nothing. If you'd rather see it before you talk about it, the showroom is open.
